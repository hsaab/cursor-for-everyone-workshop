# Internal Call — Q2 Revenue & Capacity Review

**Date:** June 11, 2026
**Attendees:** Jordan Walsh (VP, Finance), Casey Brennan (Finance Director), Riley Foster (Director, Product — Compute), Avery Dunn (Revenue Operations)

---

**Jordan:** Thanks everyone. Quick one before the board prep. Numbers look great — June is tracking to about 415 million, up almost 10 percent month over month. No complaints there.

**Casey:** Yeah, the topline is healthy. The problem is the question I keep getting from leadership. Every single review, someone asks "okay but *which GPUs* are actually driving the revenue?" and right now I can't answer it from the dashboard. The dashboard only shows the total and the regional split.

**Riley:** That's the question that matters though, right? Because the mix is changing fast. The Blackwell ramp — the GB200s — that's been going vertical. Back in January that was basically a rounding error and now it's a real line.

**Casey:** Exactly. So every month I'm pulling the raw numbers into a spreadsheet by hand to slice it by GPU type. It's a CSV export, I pivot it, I make a chart, I paste it into a doc. Takes me half a day and it's stale the moment I finish.

**Jordan:** So what would actually help you?

**Casey:** Honestly? Get the GPU-type breakdown *into* the dashboard so it's just there. Revenue by GPU type — A100, L40S, H100, H200, GB200. So I can see at a glance which ones are the big earners.

**Riley:** And the trend over time, not just this month. The story leadership cares about is the shift — the older A100s tapering off while Blackwell takes over. If you only show June you miss the whole narrative.

**Casey:** Right. I want to see the last six months so the mix shift is obvious. Ideally something that flags the fastest-growing type, and the moment a newer generation overtakes an older one. That GB200-overtaking-A100 crossover is exactly the kind of thing the board lights up about.

**Avery:** From RevOps side — the data's all there, it's clean. It's the same numbers feeding billing, just at the GPU-type grain. The issue is purely that nobody's surfaced it in the dashboard yet.

**Jordan:** Okay. And for Thursday — I don't need the dashboard for the board, I need a few clean slides. Branded. The headline revenue, the breakdown by GPU type, the mix-shift trend, and a couple of takeaways. Can we get there?

**Casey:** If the breakdown existed in the dashboard, turning it into slides would be the easy part. That's the whole ask really — get it out of my spreadsheet and into something repeatable.

**Jordan:** Perfect. Let's make that happen.
